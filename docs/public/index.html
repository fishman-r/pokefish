Cozy pixel fish for your pocket.

Game Overview

Raise

Feed fish with limited food stock, watch their appetite, and collect idle pond rewards.

Hatch

Claim a daily fish egg, choose an egg to open, and discover new companions.

Collect

Unlock species and evolved forms in a compact handheld-style aquarium catalog.

中文简介

Pokefish 是一款竖屏像素风养鱼小游戏。玩家可以投喂伙伴、收取水域资源、领取每日鱼蛋、孵化新鱼，并在图鉴中慢慢收集不同鱼类和进化形态。

Pokefish is a local-save iPhone game. No accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking are used in the current version.
