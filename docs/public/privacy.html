Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-06-13

Pokefish is a simple mobile game about raising pixel fish in a local aquarium.

Data Collection

Pokefish does not collect personal data.

The current version does not use user accounts, advertising, analytics SDKs, tracking SDKs, third-party login, camera access, microphone access, photo library access, location access, or contacts access.

Local Game Data

Pokefish saves gameplay progress locally on your device. This may include fish companions, resources, hatchery progress, fish catalog progress, feeding state, and other gameplay values.

This local save data is used only to keep your game progress. It is not uploaded by Pokefish to a server. If you delete the app, local game progress may be deleted as well.

Children

Pokefish is designed as a gentle casual game. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If Pokefish adds online features, analytics, ads, accounts, cloud save, or other data collection in the future, this policy and the App Store privacy details must be updated before release.

Contact

Support contact: https://github.com/fishman-r/pokefish/issues
