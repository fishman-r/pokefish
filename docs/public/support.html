Support information for the iPhone version.

About

Pokefish is a portrait mobile game about raising pixel fish, hatching eggs, evolving companions, and filling an aquarium journal.

Common Questions

Is Pokefish online?
The current version is designed as an offline local-save game.

Where is my progress saved?
Progress is saved locally on your device. Deleting the app may delete your progress.

Does Pokefish collect personal data?
The current version does not collect personal data, use accounts, show ads, or track users.

Bug Reports

If you find a bug, please include your device model, iOS version, what you tapped before the issue happened, and a screenshot if possible.
